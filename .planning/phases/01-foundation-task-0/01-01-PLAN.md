| T-01-SC | Tampering | Gradle/Spring dependency supply chain | accept | All dependencies are first-party Spring/Flyway/Testcontainers/Postgres from Maven Central; no npm/pip/cargo installs in this plan, so no package-legitimacy gate required. |
