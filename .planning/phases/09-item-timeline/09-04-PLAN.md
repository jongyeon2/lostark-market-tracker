<objective>
Build the **focal point of the whole project**: the price line chart with event markers overlaid (TIME-02/03/04). This is the headline visualization — a single chart that reads "the price moved around 로아ON / 대형 업데이트" by drawing per-`eventType` vertical markers over the `min_price` line, plus the honest downsample signal.

Three engineering signals land here: (1) **off-by-9h discipline in the axis** — positions on raw UTC epoch, labels in KST, never a hand-shifted instant; (2) **honest downsampling made visual** — raw points carry dots, bucket averages don't, and a badge + tooltip say "you're looking at a {bucket} average, not raw" (the chart extension of the project's honest-data ethos); (3) **correlation, not causation** — markers show temporal overlap; the copy never claims the event caused the move (Phase 10 carries that notice).

The Recharts coexistence wrinkle (one Tooltip per chart) is resolved deterministically: the price line uses a custom Tooltip `content`, and marker hover uses a `ReferenceLine` label with a native SVG `<title>` — two independent hover affordances, no conflict.

Output: `PriceTimelineChart.tsx` + `EventMarkerLegend.tsx` + `DownsampleBadge.tsx`.
</objective>
